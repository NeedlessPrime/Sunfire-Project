01  The idea

Not a team. Not a vote. The Sun.

Most tokens move when someone decides they should. SunFire doesn't. Its supply shrinks when the Sun erupts, and only then.

It is the first crypto literally connected to space: the trigger is a measurement taken 36,000 km above Earth by NOAA's GOES satellites, and the result is a burn on Solana, a minute later.

8minfor a flare's light to reach Earth
36,000kmaltitude of the GOES satellites that measure it
60sbetween two checks of NOAA's data by the bot

03The burn schedule

The bigger the flare, the bigger the burn.

Each event spends a random share of the burn wallet within its tier, drawn from a recent Solana block hash. Nobody picks it.

M-class flare

5–10%

of the burn wallet. Common: several a month near solar maximum.

X-class flare

10–25%

of the burn wallet. The strongest flares, ten times an M.

G4+ storm

30–50%

of the burn wallet. Severe geomagnetic storms, the kind that light auroras far south.

The fuel

50%

of every creator fee stays in the burn wallet, waiting for the next eruption.

04How it works

Three steps, zero humans.

From a flare on the Sun to tokens destroyed on Solana, every step is automatic, public and checkable.

Fees fill the burn wallet

Every trade of $SFIRE pays creator fees. They flow to the public burn wallet: half goes to the team, half waits for the Sun.

pump.fun · creator fees

The Sun erupts

NOAA's GOES satellites record an M or X-class flare, or a G4+ geomagnetic storm. The bot reads NOAA's feeds every minute.

$SFIRE burns

The bot buys $SFIRE on Jupiter with a share of the burn wallet and burns every token, with a public memo naming the NOAA event.

05The tech

Built like a satellite.

Space data in, Solana transactions out. The bot is open source, and every action it takes is on-chain.

Space data, straight from NOAA

The official GOES flare list and planetary Kp index from NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center. Each flare is named by its NOAA start time.

Solana speed

Buybacks and burns settle in seconds for a fraction of a cent, so even a small flare can burn.

Best route through Jupiter

Every buyback is routed through Jupiter, with a price impact cap so a thin pool never wastes a burn.

Signed with care

Only allowlisted programs, SOL only into the bot's own account, and a simulation of every swap before anything is signed.

Every action on-chain

Each buyback, burn and fee share carries a memo naming its NOAA event. One event, one burn, never twice.

100 % open source

The bot, its tests and this website live in one public repository. Read every rule, line by line.
