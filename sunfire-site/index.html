02  The idea

Not humans. A star.

Most tokens move when someone decides they should. SunFire doesn't. Its supply shrinks when the Sun erupts, and only then. No team picks the date, and no one picks the size.

A flare 150 million kilometres away hits NOAA's satellites, and minutes later $SFIRE is bought back and burned on Solana.

“The only thing that can burn $SFIRE is the Sun.”

No presaleFair launch.

Launched on Pump.fun's public bonding curve, with no presale and no private round.

No scheduleEvent driven.

Burns happen when real solar events happen. Quiet Sun, no burn.

No secretsOpen source.

Every line of the code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check what it does.

How it works

Three steps. That's it.

The tech is deliberately simple. Fewer moving parts, fewer places for anything to hide.

01

The Sun flares.

NOAA's GOES satellites measure the Sun's X-rays every minute. M and X-class flares and G4+ geomagnetic storms are the triggers.

02

The bot writes it on-chain.

The SunFire bot publishes each NOAA reading to Solana with its timestamp. Anyone can compare it with NOAA's public data.

03

The program buys and burns.

The program checks the event, sizes the burn, buys $SFIRE through Jupiter with the burn vault and burns every token bought.

The burn schedule

Bigger events, bigger burns.

Each event spends a share of the burn vault. The exact share is drawn within its range when the event is confirmed.

The fuel

Pump.fun's fee sharing pays half of the creator fees straight to the public burn wallet. The Sun decides when it gets spent.

Rule · public on Solana One event, one burn.

Every buyback is tagged on Solana with the NOAA event it answers, and an event is never bought twice. A flare that grows from M to X counts once for each tier.

What the bot does
  • Fixed sharesEach event spends a random share of the burn wallet within its tier's range, never more.
  • Everything bought is burnedEvery $SFIRE the bot buys through Jupiter is burned right after, with a public memo.
  • No duplicatesEach buyback names its NOAA event, and an event already bought is skipped.
  • Public walletEvery buy and burn of the burn wallet can be checked on a Solana explorer.
  • Holders untouchedTokens in your wallet are never touched.

Good to know.

  1. Solar Cycle 25 peaked around 2024–2025. X-class flares will get rarer in the coming years, while M-class flares stay common.
  2. Burns are run by the SunFire bot from a public wallet. Its code is open and every action is on Solana, but no smart contract stops that wallet from being used otherwise: holders trust the team on that.
  3. Events come from NOAA's official flare list and planetary Kp index. Each flare is identified by its NOAA start time.
  4. Burn sizes are drawn from a recent Solana block hash, not from verifiable randomness.
  5. SunFire is an experimental token. Nothing on this site is financial advice.
